## Bio

I believe technology should be used to build for good.

My work is centered around helping people use technology to create meaningful, real-world impact — whether that's enabling developers to move faster, supporting students and founders, or building solutions that solve problems in thoughtful ways.

I'm a Cloud Advocate at Microsoft, focused on AI, developer tools, and modern application development. I help developers build and modernize applications using tools like GitHub Copilot, sharing practical approaches through hands-on workshops, demos, and conference talks.

I regularly speak at major developer conferences including JavaOne, DevNexus, JDConf, and Microsoft Build. I was named to the Forbes 30 Under 30 (Seattle) list and have been featured in GeekWire and KOMO News for my work at the intersection of technology and community impact.

Beyond Microsoft, I'm an active open source contributor — including contributions to LangChain4j and Spring AI — and I'm passionate about mentoring students, supporting founders, and building projects that prioritize impact alongside innovation.
